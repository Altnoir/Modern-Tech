# The Processing Table

Machines are assembled on the **Processing Table** using a casing and the parts required by the recipe.

<Recipe id="moderntech:processing_table/processing_table" />

Place the casing in its separate slot and fill the grid cells shown in the recipe with the required parts. The parts may be placed in any of those cells, but each recipe cell must be filled and the quantities must match the recipe.

<Recipe id="moderntech:steam_age/bronze/machine_casing_asbl" />

The machine type determines how many parts its recipe needs. The recipe viewer shows one valid arrangement; you may swap the parts within the recipe cells. To upgrade many machines, craft the existing machine with the next tier's machine hull.
