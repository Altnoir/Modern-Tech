# Water Barrel

Place 27 Water Barrels in a solid 3×3×3 cube. The cube forms automatically, generates 1,000,000 mB of water per tick, and stores up to 2,147,483,647 mB.

Right-click any exposed barrel to check the water level, use an empty bucket to collect water, or connect a pipe to an outer face to extract it. If you break an outer barrel, replace it to resume production with the remaining water. Breaking the center barrel empties the tank.

Craft one Wax from one Honeycomb. Add it to a Barrel, or place it in the center of the vanilla Barrel recipe.
